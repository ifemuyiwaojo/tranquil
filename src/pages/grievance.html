Georgia Healthcare Facility Regulation

Private home care providers are licensed by the Georgia Department of Community Health. You may file a complaint with or without telling us first.

  • Complaint intake: (800) 878-6442 or (404) 657-5726
  • Private Home Care program: (404) 657-5700
  • Email: hfrd.phcp@dch.ga.gov
  • Online: dch.georgia.gov/hfrd-file-complaint
  • Healthcare Facility Regulation Division, 2 Martin Luther King Jr. Drive SE, East Tower, Atlanta, GA 30334

Privacy complaints can also go to the U.S. Department of Health and Human Services Office for Civil Rights. The privacy notice explains that path.
