About

Care that lets people stay home.

Tranquil, Inc. is a licensed private home care provider in Kennesaw and has been providing these services for twenty-five years, since 2001. Over these years, clients and families have come to trust the work of Tranquil, Inc.

Our mission

To provide quality, professional, timely, and cost-effective personal care and skilled nursing for people of every age, including children, older adults, people with disabilities, and people with intellectual disabilities.

Our vision

The best of skilled nursing, respite, companionship, homemaking, and personal care, so people can live in their own homes or with their families in a dignified and appreciably independent way and manner.

Our goal

Efficient, affordable home care for young and older people, including children, regardless of the health condition that brings them to us. Our services are careful. Our private-pay rates are competitive.

Licensed

Private home care in Georgia is licensed by the Department of Community Health. Tranquil, Inc. provides nursing, personal care, and companion or sitter services in the home.

Nurse-led

A nurse assesses, plans, and supervises. Caregivers are oriented to the person they are supporting, not sent in with a generic task list.

One agency, many ages

Children, adults with disabilities, and older adults can all be served here when the program and the plan of care allow it.

What we will not promise

We will not tell you that a waiver is approved before the state says it is. We will not describe emergency care on this website. If someone is in danger, call 911.

Eligibility for CCSP, SOURCE, ICWP, and other waivers is decided by the case management agency, not by Tranquil. We can help you understand the next step and, when you are authorized, provide the care.

What makes Tranquil, Inc. different

We take pride in high-quality home health care for clients with varying health conditions and care requirements, and in the best outcomes we can support. More than two decades without a negative report or evaluation from the regulatory agencies attests to the consistently excellent performance of Tranquil, Inc. as a home health care provider.

Our services are excellent and our rates are competitive. We bring quality, professional, and affordable personal care to you and your loved ones in the comfort of your home.
