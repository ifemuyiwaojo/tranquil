Programs

How the care is paid for.

Tranquil, Inc. accepts clients through the programs below, and through private pay. A case manager or the payer decides eligibility and the hours of service. Tranquil, Inc. provides the authorized services.

CCSP

The Community Care Services Program is part of Georgia’s Elderly and Disabled Waiver Programs. It serves people who would otherwise qualify for nursing-facility care, with personal support, respite, and related services at home or in the community.

To apply, contact Georgia’s Aging and Disability Resource Connection, the Area Agency on Aging. The area agencies are listed by county on the state Medicaid site.

SOURCE

SOURCE is the other branch of the same waiver as CCSP. A primary care physician is part of the team, and enhanced case management coordinates the services. Personal support at home can be part of the plan.

ICWP

The Independent Care Waiver Program serves a limited number of adults who apply between ages 21 and 64 and who have a severe physical disability or traumatic brain injury. Services are for people at a nursing-facility or hospital level of care.

Applications go through the state’s ICWP intake, not through a home care agency. Tranquil, Inc. can explain how to apply to the appropriate government agency and what personal support looks like once a client is enrolled.

Intellectual and developmental disability waivers

Tranquil also serves people with intellectual disabilities when a waiver authorizes personal support in the home. The state, not the agency, decides which waiver applies.

CareSource

CareSource members can be served when Tranquil is authorized for that member’s services. Call us with the member’s plan details and we will tell you whether we can start.

Long-term care insurance

Many long-term care insurance policies pay for home care. Tranquil, Inc. will review the benefit summary with you. Approval still belongs to the insurance company.

Private pay

Clients can arrange care directly. Rates depend on the hours and the type of service. Ask for a straightforward quote before anything starts.

Veterans

We provide home care for veterans. Some families use a VA benefit, some use a waiver, and some pay privately. Bring the benefit information you have and we will sort out what it covers.

Already receiving waiver services?

In CCSP, SOURCE, and ICWP, members can ask to receive services from a different provider. Hours come from the care plan, not from the agency. If you want to move to Tranquil, call us before you cancel anyone. We coordinate the start date with your case manager so there is no gap.

Official program descriptions live on Georgia Medicaid’s waiver page.
