Privacy

Notice of privacy practices

Effective November 13, 2026. This notice describes how medical information about you may be used and disclosed, and how you can get access to this information. Please review it carefully.

Tranquil, Inc., P.O. Box 2754, Kennesaw, GA 30156, is the provider described in this notice. Questions go to the Privacy Officer at that address, at (678) 290-0070, or at tranquil2754@hotmail.com.

Our duty

We are required by law to maintain the privacy of protected health information, to give you this notice of our legal duties and privacy practices, and to notify you if a breach of unsecured protected health information occurs. We will not use or share your information other than as described here unless you tell us we may, in writing. You may change your mind later. Tell us in writing if you do.

How we may use and share information

Treatment. We use information to provide personal care and nursing, to write and update a plan of care, and to coordinate with your physicians, waiver case managers, and the family members involved in your care.

Payment. We use information to bill Medicaid waivers, CareSource, long-term care insurance, or you, and to obtain authorization for services.

Health care operations. We use information to supervise caregivers, train staff, review the quality of care, and run the agency.

We may also share information when the law requires it, for public health, for health oversight, to report abuse when we are required to, in response to a court or lawful process, to avert a serious threat, and for workers’ compensation as the law allows. Other uses, including most marketing, require your written authorization.

Your rights

  • You may ask to see or get a copy of your record. We may charge a reasonable copy cost.
  • You may ask us to correct information you believe is wrong or incomplete.
  • You may ask for a list of certain disclosures we made of your information.
  • You may ask us to limit what we use or share. We are not required to agree, except where the law says we must.
  • You may ask us to contact you in a particular way, for example at a different phone number.
  • You may ask for a paper copy of this notice.
  • If you pay for a service in full out of pocket, you may ask us not to share information about that service with your health plan, where the law gives you that right.

This website

Forms on this website are encrypted in your browser before they are transmitted. The site does not use advertising trackers, and it does not send your answers to a form-collection company. Do not put medical details in an ordinary email if you can use a form instead. Calling the office is also fine.

Complaints

If you believe your privacy rights have been violated, you may complain to the Privacy Officer at the address above. You may also file a complaint with the U.S. Department of Health and Human Services Office for Civil Rights at hhs.gov/hipaa/filing-a-complaint. We will not retaliate against you for filing a complaint.

We may change this notice. The current notice will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

This notice is the agency’s public statement of its privacy practices. Have it reviewed with the agency’s privacy advisor as part of the HIPAA program. The website encryption is one safeguard. It does not replace policies, training, or a risk analysis.
