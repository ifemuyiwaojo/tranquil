What we do

Care that belongs at home.

All services

Personal care and home support

Duties include bathing, grooming, dressing, meal preparation, light housekeeping, bed making, laundry, grocery shopping, picking up prescriptions, reminders to take medications, and the other tasks on a client’s care plan, so the client stays comfortable and safe at home.

Skilled nursing

Skilled nursing when the plan of care requires it, and supervisory nursing visits when the plan of care calls for a licensed nurse.

Medications

Reminders, and administration when it is part of an authorized plan of care.

Home support

Light housekeeping, laundry, meals, groceries, and prescription pickup.

Companionship and respite

A steady presence at home, and a break for the family member who usually provides care.

Rides to appointments

Transportation to the doctor’s appointment when that ride is required and included in the service plan.

A nurse stays involved

The visit is only the start.

A nurse assesses what is needed, writes the plan of care with the client, and supervises the care in a manner consistent with Georgia personal support requirements. Tranquil, Inc. adheres to these requirements.

Payment methods

Georgia waiver programs, insurance, and private pay.

CCSP and SOURCE

Community care waiver programs for older adults and people with physical challenges who qualify for nursing-facility level of care.

ICWP

The Independent Care Waiver Program for adults with severe physical disabilities or traumatic brain injury.

Private and other coverage

Private pay, long-term care insurance, CareSource when authorized, and care for veterans.

How care begins

A clear path from the first call.

Step 1

Tell us what you need

Call, or send a referral. Families and case managers both use the same form.

Step 2

We confirm the fit

We look at the program, the county, and the schedule, and we quickly tell the client when services will start. If Tranquil, Inc. is not suited to provide the required services, the client is notified promptly.

Step 3

A nurse visits

The assessment happens at home. The plan of care is developed with the client and family, and it is documented.

Step 4

Care starts, and is reviewed

A suitable caregiver is matched. A nurse supervises and updates the plan of care on the regular schedule, or sooner when the need arises.
