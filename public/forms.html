Families and case managers

Request care

A referral from a family member, a client, or a care coordinator. This is the fastest way to ask Tranquil to start.

Send a message

A general question that is not a full referral and not a clinical note.

Share a concern

Tell Tranquil directly. You can also contact the state. Both paths are explained on the form.

Clinical staff

These match Tranquil’s nursing assessment and the CCSP supervisory note. A staff code is required before a clinical form can be submitted. Nurses can fill the form first and unlock it at the end.

CCSP initial assessment

First nursing assessment for Community Care Services Program personal support.

ICWP initial assessment

First nursing assessment for the Independent Care Waiver Program.

ICWP bi-monthly assessment

The follow-up nursing assessment for an ICWP client.

CCSP RN supervisory visit

Supervisory visit and progress notes, including the care coordinator communication.
