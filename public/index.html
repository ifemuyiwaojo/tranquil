What we do

Care that belongs at home.

All services

Personal care

Bathing, dressing, grooming, and the other daily tasks that keep a person comfortable and safe.

Skilled nursing

Nursing visits and supervision when the plan of care calls for a licensed nurse.

Medications

Reminders, and administration when it is part of an authorized plan of care.

Home support

Light housekeeping, laundry, meals, groceries, and prescription pickup.

Companionship and respite

A steady presence at home, and a break for the family member who usually provides care.

Rides to appointments

Transportation to the doctor when that help is part of the services arranged for you.

A nurse stays involved

The visit is only the start.

A nurse assesses what is needed, writes the plan with you, and comes back to see how the care is going. Caregivers are not left to guess.

That supervision is part of how Georgia personal support is supposed to work, and it is part of how Tranquil works.

How to pay

Waiver programs, insurance, and private pay.

CCSP and SOURCE

Community care for older adults and people with disabilities who qualify for nursing-facility level of care.

ICWP

Home support for adults with severe physical disabilities or traumatic brain injury.

Private and other coverage

Private pay, long-term care insurance, CareSource when authorized, and care for veterans.

How care begins

A clear path from the first call.

Step 1

Tell us what you need

Call, or send a referral. Families and case managers both use the same form.

Step 2

We confirm the fit

We look at the program, the county, and the schedule, and we say so if we are not the right provider.

Step 3

A nurse visits

The assessment happens at home. The plan is written with the client and family.

Step 4

Care starts, and is reviewed

A caregiver is matched. A nurse supervises and updates the plan when needs change.
