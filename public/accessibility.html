Accessibility

The site should be usable by the person who needs it.

Pages use written labels on every form field, a skip link, visible keyboard focus, and text that can be resized in the browser. If a page does not work with your screen reader, keyboard, or magnification, call (678) 290-0070 and we will take the information another way.

If you need this information in another language or in a format such as large print, call the same number and ask. Language help is free.
