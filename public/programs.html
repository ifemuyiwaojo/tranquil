Programs

How the care is paid for.

Tranquil accepts clients through the programs below, and through private pay. A case manager or the payer decides eligibility and hours. We provide the authorized services.

CCSP

The Community Care Services Program is part of Georgia’s Elderly and Disabled Waiver. It serves people who would otherwise qualify for nursing-facility care, with personal support, respite, and related services at home or in the community.

To apply, contact Georgia’s Aging and Disability Resource Connection. The area agencies are listed by county on the state Medicaid site.

SOURCE

SOURCE is the other branch of that same waiver. A primary care physician is part of the team, and enhanced case management coordinates the services. Personal support at home can be part of the plan.

ICWP

The Independent Care Waiver Program serves a limited number of adults who apply between ages 21 and 64 and who have a severe physical disability or traumatic brain injury, at a nursing-facility or hospital level of care.

Applications go through the state’s ICWP intake, not through a home care agency. We can talk with you about what personal support looks like once you are enrolled.

Intellectual and developmental disability waivers

Tranquil also serves people with intellectual disabilities when a waiver authorizes personal support in the home. The state, not the agency, decides which waiver applies.

CareSource

CareSource members can be served when Tranquil is authorized for that member’s services. Call us with the member’s plan details and we will tell you whether we can start.

Long-term care insurance

Many long-term care policies pay for home care. We will review the benefit summary with you. Approval still belongs to the insurance company.

Private pay

You can arrange care directly. Rates depend on the hours and the type of help. Ask for a straightforward quote before anything starts.

Veterans

We provide home care for veterans. Some families use a VA benefit, some use a waiver, and some pay privately. Bring the benefit information you have and we will sort out what it covers.

Already receiving waiver services?

In CCSP, SOURCE, and ICWP, members can ask to receive services from a different provider. Hours come from the care plan, not from the agency. If you want to move to Tranquil, call us before you cancel anyone. We coordinate the start date with your case manager so there is no gap.

Official program descriptions live on Georgia Medicaid’s waiver page.
