Services

The care on the brochure, explained plainly.

The standard home-delivered services are listed below. What any client receives depends on the nursing assessment, the plan of care, and the rules of the program paying for it.

Personal care

Hands-on help with bathing, dressing, grooming, and bed making, done in a way that protects the client’s dignity.

Skilled nursing

Licensed nursing services as written in the plan of care, including the supervisory visits required in Georgia for personal support.

Specialized health support

The extra clinical attention some clients need at home, when it is authorized by the nurse’s license and stated in the care plan.

Medication reminders and administration

Reminders to take medicines. Administration only when the plan of care and Georgia rules allow a caregiver or nurse to do it.

Adult companion and sitter

Company, supervision, and a calm presence for someone who should not be alone.

Respite

A planned break for the family caregiver, so the person receiving care is not left without support.

Light housekeeping and laundry

The ordinary upkeep of the home that is part of personal support, not a separate cleaning service.

Light cooking

Meals prepared in the home according to the person’s diet and the plan of care.

Errands

Grocery shopping and picking up prescriptions.

Transportation

Rides to doctor’s appointments when transportation is part of the arranged services.

Veterans

Home care for veterans. Ask us what a benefit, a waiver, or private pay can cover in your situation. We do not decide VA eligibility.

Children and people with disabilities

Services for children, people with physical disabilities, and people with intellectual disabilities, when a program authorizes that care at home.

Who comes to the house

Personal support aides and companions provide the daily services. A registered nurse, or a licensed practical nurse under RN supervision, provides the nursing oversight. The exact mix is set at the assessment.
