Careers

The work is in someone’s home. Treat it that way.

Tranquil hires personal support aides, companions and sitters, and licensed nurses for CCSP, ICWP, and private-pay clients across our Georgia counties.

Start the application

Personal support aide

Bathing, dressing, meals, light housekeeping, and the other tasks on a client’s plan. You work from that plan, and a nurse supervises the care.

Companion or sitter

Supervision, conversation, and safety for a person who needs someone present. This is not medical care unless the plan says otherwise.

LPN and RN

Assessments, supervisory visits, teaching, and the nursing tasks in the plan of care. RNs sign the clinical forms on this site.

What to have ready

The paper application asks for copies of the items that apply to the role:

  • CNA, LPN, or RN license, when the role requires one
  • Current CPR certification
  • Tuberculosis test result
  • Driver’s license
  • Social Security card

You can upload them in the secure application or mail copies to P.O. Box 2754, Kennesaw, GA 30156. A resume can stand in for the work-history pages if you say so on the form.

What the application includes

It is the full Tranquil packet in one place: the job you want, availability, driving questions, work history, the criminal records request, the statements you must read before you sign, and an optional survey that is kept apart from the hiring decision.

A criminal record is requested. It does not automatically keep you from being hired. Tranquil is an equal opportunity employer.

Employment, if offered, is at will. The application itself is not a job offer.
